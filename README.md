# 🏙️ Prosperity City — 2D Open World Financial RPG Adventure

> A playable 2D top-down financial universe where you explore an open-world city, walk into banks and trading floors, talk to colorful citizens, collect coins and wisdom scrolls, and build your wealth empire!

---

## 🎮 What is Prosperity City?

Unlike boring finance apps, **Prosperity City** puts you inside a living, breathing virtual city:
- 🕹️ **Control Your Character**: Move freely with WASD, Arrow keys, or Click-to-Walk. (On mobile, a responsive Virtual D-Pad appears).
- 🏙️ **Walkable Universe**: Explore Central Plaza, Financial District, Commercial Avenue, and Residential Quarters.
- 🏦 **Walk Into Real Buildings**:
  - **First Prosperity Bank & Vault**: Deposit into a 4.5% APY High-Yield Savings Vault, pay off loan monsters using the Debt Avalanche.
  - **Wall Street Exchange Trading Floor**: Step onto the active floor and buy index funds (S&P 500) and blue-chip shares.
  - **Prestige Auto Dealership**: Compare buying a $4,500 reliable used commuter car in cash vs signing a $620/month 5-year luxury sports car debt trap.
  - **Apex Career Tower**: Deliver high-impact projects for permanent workplace salary bumps.
  - **FinTwist University**: Solve the Rule of 72 pop quiz for a cash scholarship and XP.
  - **Your Home Sanctuary**: Rest to restore Happiness and Health, and review your balance sheet.
- 🗣️ **Wandering NPCs**: Chat with Banker Sterling, Aunt Penny, Trader Chad, and Shady Sam.
- 🪙 **Collectibles**: Hidden gold coins and wisdom scrolls scattered around the city.

